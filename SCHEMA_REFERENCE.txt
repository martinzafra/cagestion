   - deposit (yes_no_na enum), deposit_amount (DECIMAL)
   - payment_type_id (FK -> inventory_payment_types)
   - comments, guest_comments (TEXT)
   - price_basis (price_basis enum: DAY | WEEK | MONTH)
   - daily_price (DECIMAL, NOT NULL)
   - cleaning_charge, other_charge (DECIMAL, default 0)
